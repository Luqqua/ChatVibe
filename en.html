Short Overview

The Twitch Chat Analysis Tool evaluates uploaded chat data (CSV/JSON), which can be obtained from twitchchatdownloader.com or the TwitchDownloader on GitHub. It provides key information at a glance, including highlights, KPIs (e.g. activity, diversity), and frequently mentioned topics. This makes it easy to discover active phases, identify viral moments, and optimize content effectively – even cross-platform, such as creating short/ TikTok videos. More details can be found here.
